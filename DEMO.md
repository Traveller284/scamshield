# Four-minute Round 2 demo

## 0:00–0:35 — The pause

Open the Check tab and paste the SBI KYC message from the sample chips. Show the animated score, highlighted link, reasons, and plain advice. Point out that ScamShield shares only an anonymous scam type with Radar, and show the private toggle.

## 0:35–1:10 — Explain the decision

Check the Number tab. Enter a sample number or UPI ID and expand **How we decide**. Call out that this is transparent risk scoring, not an identity database. Use the message's number action to connect the two features.

## 1:10–1:50 — Protect family

Open Family Shield, create a code, show the guardian subscribe link, then join on this device. The test notification needs internet. Explain that real high-risk alerts share the scam type but never the message text.

## 1:50–2:40 — Build instincts

Play the Scam or Safe? round and ask ScamShield to check one sample directly from the question. Switch to Red-Flag Hunt and tap the urgent words before revealing the answer. Show XP and levels.

## 2:40–3:25 — Community picture

Open Scam Radar and show the scam-type chart and city counts. Return to Check and turn on private mode to demonstrate that the next check is not shared.

## 3:25–4:00 — Multilingual + safety

Switch the interface language and check the Hindi sample. Finish with the limitations: rules are explainable but can miss new or mixed-language scams; a safe result is not a guarantee.

## Offline backup

The main checking, number scoring, family screens, learning modes, and locally stored radar work without external AI. OCR, Grok, ntfy notifications, phishing feeds, and Telegram need internet. If a service is unavailable, explain the local flow and show its setup or status message rather than claiming it worked.
